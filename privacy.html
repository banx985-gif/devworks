DEVWORKS Privacy Policy

DEVWORKS is a game by Banx Games. Last updated 14 November 2026.

In short: your studios are saved on your phone only. DEVWORKS has no account and no game server, and we (Banx Games) collect nothing about you. The ads come from Google AdMob and purchases go through Google Play, under Google's own privacy rules.

What DEVWORKS keeps, and where

  • Your saves (your studios, staff, games, settings, achievements and Hall of Fame) are stored on your device only. They never leave it.
  • Names you type (your studio's name, your director's name, your games' titles) stay in those saves on your device. They are never sent anywhere or shown to anyone else.
  • You can delete everything by deleting the save slots in the game, clearing the app's data, or uninstalling DEVWORKS.

What we collect

Nothing. DEVWORKS has no account, no login, no game server, no online leaderboard and no analytics of our own. We can't see your saves or anything you do in the game.

Ads (Google AdMob)

DEVWORKS is free and shows a few ads: ones you choose to watch for a reward, and an occasional ad between screens (none after you buy Remove Ads or the Supporter Pack). The ads come from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's ad software may use your device's advertising ID, your approximate location (from your IP address), and information about the ads shown and tapped. That is handled by Google under its own policy: how Google uses information in ads.

  • On your first start, DEVWORKS asks whether ads may be picked for you or should be general ads only. In Europe and the UK, Google's own consent form asks instead.
  • You can change your answer any time in Settings → Privacy & legal → Ad choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Purchases (Google Play)

The Supporter Pack, Remove Ads and Studio Token packs are bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. The game keeps only Google's receipt on your device, so it knows what you own.

Notifications

If you say yes when Uncle Ray asks, DEVWORKS can show a few short reminders (for example, when Awards Night is coming up). They are made and scheduled on your phone. No data is sent anywhere for them. At most three a day, never between 10 pm and 8 am, and each kind can be switched off in Settings → Notifications.

Children

DEVWORKS is a business management game made for teenagers and adults. It is not aimed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date.
